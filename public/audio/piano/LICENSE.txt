Salamander Grand Piano
======================

The .mp3 files in this directory are samples of a Yamaha C5 grand piano from
the Salamander Grand Piano sample library.

  Author:  Alexander Holm
  Source:  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  Mirror:  https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/  (the subset used here)
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

What is included
----------------
A 30-sample subset at a single velocity layer, one sample every minor third
from A0 to C8, encoded as 128 kbps mp3. Tone.js pitch-shifts between these
sample points to cover the full keyboard. The complete original library is
considerably larger and includes multiple velocity layers; this subset was
chosen so the samples can be bundled with the application rather than fetched
from a third-party host at runtime.
